【写作说明】

  1. 书信格式规范：范文严格遵循英文电子邮件的标准格式，包含称呼、正文、结尾敬语和签名，结构清晰完整。
  2. 内容完整具体：邮件正文首先礼貌回应来信，随即清晰说明了项目的目标（支持偏远地区乡村小学）、核心活动（捐赠书籍以开阔视野、培养能力）以及对志愿者的期望（勤奋、乐观），信息具体且紧扣题目要求。
  3. 语言正式得体：全文使用正式、礼貌的书信语言（如“I am exceedingly delighted to receive your letter", “please do not hesitate to contact me”），符合工作邮件沟通的语境。
  4. 逻辑衔接自然：通过“First and foremost"和”In addition"等逻辑连接词，使项目不同方面的介绍过渡自然，条理分明。
  5. 指定信息处理：正确使用"Li Ming"作为落款姓名，并提供了具体的项目联系邮箱，完全符合题目指令。

【参考范文】

Dear William,

I am exceedingly delighted to receive your letter in which you inquired about the “Aiding Rural Primary Schools” project. As a member of the project, I am writing to provide you with some essential information.

First and foremost, the project aims to support rural primary schools in remote areas through various activities. For example, we send books on a wide range of subjects to the students, which not only broadens their horizons and enriches their lives, but also helps them develop the ability to think and learn independently and critically. In addition, we look for volunteers who are diligent and have an optimistic and pleasant personality. Such qualities help ensure that they maintain a positive attitude when facing challenges during the project.

If you have any further questions, please do not hesitate to contact me at aidingruralprimaryschools@edu.cn.

Yours sincerely, Li Ming
