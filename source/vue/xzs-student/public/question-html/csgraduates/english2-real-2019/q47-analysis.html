【归纳要点】

  1. 格式：书信（建议信）。
  2. 语域：收信人为教授，用语需正式、礼貌。
  3. 内容要点：
    • 提出一个具体的辩论主题。
    • 阐述选择该主题的理由。
    • 告知辩论活动的具体安排。

【参考范文】

Dear Professor Smith,

Thank you for trusting me to arrange the debate. As the competition is nearly ready, I am writing to suggest a topic and outline the plan.

I propose the topic: “Is Traffic Jam Primarily Caused by Inadequate Transport Infrastructure or Poor Management?” This topic is highly debatable, touching on key urban issues. It also connects with course content on urban planning and public policy, helping students deepen their understanding. Additionally, it involves current concerns like shared bikes, which can stimulate active participation.

The debate will be held in the school hall this Friday, from 6:00 p.m. to 8:00 p.m. I look forward to your feedback.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

【写作储备】

1. 开篇常用表达（寒暄并说明目的）

  • I am honored that you have asked for my input on…
  • I believe I have a good understanding of… and would like to offer some suggestions.

2. 中间段常用表达（提出建议及理由）

  • If I may suggest, I think… would be an excellent topic because…
  • It would be beneficial to choose… as it relates closely to…
  • I believe… will encourage deeper thinking and lively discussion.

3. 结尾常用表达（表达期待与协助意愿）

  • Thank you for considering my suggestions. Please let me know your thoughts.
  • I am happy to discuss this further if you would like more details.
  • Please feel free to let me know if you need any additional assistance.
