【要点归纳】

  1. 文体类型：告示。写作时需确保格式正确、完整。
  2. 核心目的：招募志愿者。内容应具有吸引力与号召力。
  3. 主要内容：
    • 简要介绍夏令营活动内容，突出其意义与特色；
    • 说明志愿者的职责与要求；
    • 提供报名或联系方式。

【范文参考】

Recruitment Announcement

We are looking for volunteers for the upcoming summer camp for high school students. The camp aims to enhance participants’ academic abilities and develop their teamwork and practical skills through various activities such as reading salons and entrepreneurship competitions.

Volunteers will assist in organizing activities and managing daily operations. Ideal candidates should be patient, proactive, warm-hearted, and responsible. Relevant experience is preferred. Interested students please contact us at studentsunion@abcuniversity.edu.cn or call 12345678.

Students’ Union

【写作储备】

一、夏令营的意义

  • make new friends 结交新朋友
  • learn new skills 学习新技能
  • discover and grow 发现与成长
  • expand one’s horizon 开阔视野
  • learn to be independent 学会独立

二、招募类表达句型

  1. 开头引入，说明目的

    • We are seeking volunteers to work with us in…
    • We plan to recruit… for…
    • Since its establishment in…, the program has become a leading activity in…
    • This role offers a challenging, stimulating and rewarding experience.

  2. 说明要求与条件

    • To be considered for…, you will ideally possess…
    • Candidates for this position should have…
    • Volunteers are expected to…

  3. 提供联系方式

    • If you are interested, please send your resume to…
    • For further information, feel free to contact…
