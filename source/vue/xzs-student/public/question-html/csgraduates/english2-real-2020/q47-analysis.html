【归纳要点】

  1. 格式：书信（“介绍 + 建议”信）。
  2. 语域：收信人为留学生，用语不必过于正式，保持礼貌、亲切即可。
  3. 内容要点：
    • ① 简要介绍古迹信息；
    • ② 提供相关旅行建议。

【参考范文】

Dear Friends,

I’m so delighted that you trust me to arrange your trip. I’m writing to inform you of the historical attraction we will visit—the Terracotta Army, one of the greatest archaeological discoveries of all time—and to share some travel tips with you.

Located to the east of downtown Xi’an, the Terracotta Army is a massive collection of life-size clay warriors and horses buried in the tomb of China’s First Emperor. It offers a fascinating glimpse into the country’s glorious past.

To help you better appreciate these breathtaking sculptures, I suggest avoiding Chinese public holidays if possible. If you must visit on those days, it’s best to set out early. Additionally, I recommend renting a multilingual audio guide at the ticket office, which will surely enrich your experience.

I hope this trip suits your taste and that you all have a wonderful time.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

【写作储备】

建议信常用表达

  1. 开篇段：说明写作目的

    • I am writing this letter on behalf of… to make some suggestions with regard to…
    • I am very delighted to share with you some of my thoughts on…
    • I have received your letter saying that… Perhaps the following suggestions are helpful for you.

  2. 中间段：介绍详情，罗列建议

    • I hereby suggest that… Also,… might be useful. I am sure if…, you will…
    • To begin with, it would be beneficial if…, because… Besides, it is highly recommended that… Last but not least, I propose that…, which will…
    • By the way, you can do some Internet research to find out the… that suits your particular needs most.

  3. 结尾段：表达期待

    • Hopefully my suggestions are constructive and please feel free to contact me if you require additional information.
    • I hope you will find these proposals practical and wish you…
