一、审题谋篇

本题要求写一封正式邀请信，邀请外籍教师担任英语演讲比赛的评委。写作时需注意以下几点：

  • 格式规范：采用正式书信格式，包括称呼、正文、结尾敬语及署名。
  • 内容完整：需清晰说明活动信息（时间、地点、性质），并礼貌表达邀请意愿。
  • 语言风格：语气应礼貌、诚恳，体现尊重与期待，避免强求。
  • 字数控制：正文约100词，需简洁明了地传达关键信息。

二、参考范文

Dear Professor William,

I am writing on behalf of our college to cordially invite you to serve as a judge for the upcoming English Speech Contest.

The contest will be held on Saturday, June 4th, at 7:00 PM in Room 102 of the Teaching Building. As a highly respected teacher among our students, your expertise and feedback would be immensely valuable to all participants.

We sincerely hope that you are available and willing to join us. Please let us know at your earliest convenience.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

三、常用句型

  1. I am writing on behalf of… to invite you to…
  2. We would be grateful if you could…
  3. We sincerely hope that you can join us and share your insights.
  4. Your presence would be a great honor for us.
  5. We look forward to your positive response.
