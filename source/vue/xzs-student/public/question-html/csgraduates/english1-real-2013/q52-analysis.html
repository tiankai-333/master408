一、审题谋篇

本题为考研英语图画作文，已连续第14年考查。图中描绘了一群“毕业生”站在人生岔路口，面临“考研”“考公”“留学”“创业”等不同方向的选择。文字提示为“选择”，属于考研写作中常见的“人生哲理”类话题。

根据题目要求，文章应包含以下三个部分：

  1. 简要描述图画：概括画面内容，包括人物和不同道路的象征意义。
  2. 阐释寓意：明确图画主题，指出“选择”对人生发展的重要性，并可在段首设置主题句，采用“总—分—总”结构展开论述。
  3. 个人评论：可围绕“选择的意义”“选择与坚持的关系”或“如何做出明智选择”等方面展开，提出自己的观点或建议。

二、参考范文

Emerging from the cartoon is an eye-catching scene in which a group of graduates stand at a critical crossroads, facing multiple paths labeled “postgraduate studies,” “civil service exam,” “studying abroad,” and “entrepreneurship.”

The implication echoed by this drawing can be summarized as a philosophical theme in our daily life: one’s future is profoundly shaped by the choices one makes. While making a wise decision is crucial to setting a clear direction, I firmly believe that persistence is equally important. Only by adhering to our goals and striving diligently can we truly achieve success. Unfortunately, some individuals tend to wander aimlessly or seek shortcuts, rather than committing to a path with determination. In reality, it is those who are both decisive and persistent who are most likely to reach their goals.

Many people argue that the key to success lies in making the right choice. However, I would suggest that graduates—and everyone else—should not only make thoughtful choices but also work tirelessly to turn their decisions into reality.
