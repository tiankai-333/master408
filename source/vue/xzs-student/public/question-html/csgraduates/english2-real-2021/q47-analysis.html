解析

一、归纳要点

  1. 格式：书信（邀请信）。
  2. 语域：面向国际学生及同辈的公务信函，应使用正式语体，同时保持礼貌亲切。
  3. 内容要点：
    • 邀请收信人参加线上会议；
    • 说明会议的具体安排与细节。

二、行文思路

  • 第一段：表明写作目的，发出邀请，并简要介绍会议的基本信息（时间、方式等）。
  • 第二段：阐述会议主题、目的及具体内容，鼓励对方参与交流。
  • 第三段：表达期待，并附上相关安排供参考。

三、参考范文

Dear Jack,

As an organizer of our monthly reading session, I am writing to invite you to our online meeting scheduled for next Friday from 2 p.m. to 4 p.m. via Zoom.

With the theme of “A Famous Writer from Your Country,” this meeting aims to expose participants to writers and works from diverse cultures. Attendees from various backgrounds will gather to engage in cross-cultural exchanges and deepen their understanding of literature. As a literary enthusiast, you are highly welcome to share your insights into the differences between English and Chinese literature.

The meeting arrangements are attached for your reference. We would be very pleased if you could accept our invitation.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

四、写作储备

1. 开篇段常用表达（说明写作目的）

  • On behalf of…, I am writing to extend to you an official invitation to…
  • We sincerely invite you to attend… located at (place) on (date).

2. 中间段常用表达（活动内容与安排）

  • Hosted by…, this meeting is aimed at…
  • During the meeting, participants will…

3. 结尾段常用表达（表达期待）

  • We will be greatly honored if you could attend our meeting.
  • An early reply would be much appreciated. We look forward to your attendance.
