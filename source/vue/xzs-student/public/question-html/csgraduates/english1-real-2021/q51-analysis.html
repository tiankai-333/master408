【写作思路解析】

一、行文思路

  1. 首段：表达祝贺，点明写作目的——为对方在华求职提供建议。
  2. 主体段：分条列举具体可行的求职建议。
  3. 结尾段：表达祝愿，传递友好期待。

二、范文

Dear Friend,

Congratulations on your graduation! I’m delighted to hear you plan to pursue a career in China. Here are some suggestions that might help.

First, clarify your career direction by considering your education, strengths, and interests. Then, research industries and companies that match your goals. Second, make use of recruitment websites—including those tailored for foreigners in China—to find job openings and submit applications online. Third, remember to apply for a post-study work visa, which is essential for working legally in China.

I hope these tips are useful. Wishing you every success in your job search!

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

三、篇章结构分析

  • 首段（2句）
    首句表达祝贺，次句表明来信目的，自然引出下文建议。

  • 主体段（4句）
    采用“首先—其次—最后”结构，分别提出三项建议：

    1. 明确职业方向，做好信息搜集；
    2. 利用招聘网站（包括外籍人士专用平台）获取信息并投递简历；
    3. 办理工作签证，强调其必要性。

  • 结尾段（1句）
    以祝愿作结，简洁有力，体现友好关怀。
