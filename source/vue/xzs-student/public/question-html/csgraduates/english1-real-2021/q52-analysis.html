【行文思路】

  1. 第一段：简要描述图片内容。
  2. 第二段：揭示图片寓意，展开分析论证。
  3. 第三段：总结观点，发出呼吁。

【范文】

① In the picture, a girl dressed in traditional opera costume tells her father that many classmates find learning opera uninteresting. Her father encouragingly replies, “Aren’t you yourself fond of it? That is all that matters.”

② The illustration conveys a meaningful message: we should pursue our passions regardless of others’ opinions. ③ The true value of a hobby or dream lies not in pleasing others, but in enriching our own lives. ④ Therefore, there is no need to hesitate in doing what we love simply due to external judgment. ⑤ Admittedly, constructive advice can aid our growth. ⑥ However, if we are easily swayed by biased or casual remarks, we risk losing control of our own path.

⑦ In summary, having a unique interest is nothing to be ashamed of. ⑧ As the saying goes, “Different strokes for different folks.” ⑨ Rather than blindly following the crowd, we should have the courage to stay true to what we genuinely love.

【篇章结构解析】

  • 第一段（2句）：
    ①句间接引述女孩的话，②句直接引用父亲的回应，形成自然对话顺序，生动呈现图片场景。

  • 第二段（4句）：
    ②句点明图片寓意，引出核心观点。③④句通过因果逻辑（“lies not in…but in…”，“Therefore…”）正面阐释观点。⑤⑥句采用让步转折结构（“Admittedly…However…”），并引入条件假设（“if we…”），增强论证深度。

  • 第三段（3句）：
    ⑦句总结观点，⑧句引用俗语补充理由，⑨句通过取舍结构（“Rather than…we should…”）发出积极呼吁，强化主题。
