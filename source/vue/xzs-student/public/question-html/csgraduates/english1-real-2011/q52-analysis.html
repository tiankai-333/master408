一、审题谋篇

本次作文围绕社会持续关注的“环境保护”主题展开。该话题曾在1991年、1999年及2000年的考研英语中出现，因此对考生而言并不陌生。此外，2010年考查的“文化”主题与2002年一致，说明话题复现是常见现象，这也提醒考生复习历年真题的重要性。

建议采用“总—分—总”结构行文：

  • 第一段：图画描述
    简要描述图画内容，紧扣主题。首句可概括图画主旨或直接点明观点；随后具体描写画面细节，如游客行为与环境污染状况；末句可再次点题或承上启下。

  • 第二段：阐释意义
    分析现象成因或影响。可从直接/间接原因（如环保意识薄弱、管理机制不健全）或潜在后果（如生态破坏）等角度展开，列举2-3个论点。

  • 第三段：个人评论
    提出解决建议或措施，如加强宣传教育、完善法规执行等，强调社会共同努力的重要性。

二、参考范文

The cartoon depicts a distressing scene where two tourists are chatting and eating on a boat, casually discarding waste into a lake already cluttered with floating rubbish. This illustration highlights a severe lack of environmental awareness among some individuals.

Nowadays, although public consciousness regarding environmental protection is growing, unpleasant phenomena such as littering in scenic areas remain common. Several factors contribute to this issue. Primarily, some people still do not take environmental protection seriously, considering littering a trivial matter. Moreover, inadequate management mechanisms—such as the absence of strict regulations or ineffective enforcement—aggravate the situation.

To address this problem, urgent measures must be implemented. Firstly, educational campaigns should be promoted in communities and schools to raise awareness. Secondly, governments ought to establish and enforce stricter environmental regulations. Only through collective effort can we preserve clean and beautiful surroundings.
