一、审题谋篇

本题要求写一封推荐信，向朋友推荐一部喜欢的电影并说明理由。写作时需注意以下几点：

  • 内容要点：明确写信对象（朋友），写信目的（推荐电影），并给出至少两个推荐理由。
  • 结构安排：建议采用三段式结构：
    1. 第一段：开门见山，表明写信目的，点明推荐的电影。
    2. 第二段：具体阐述推荐理由，可从2–3个方面展开。
    3. 第三段：再次推荐，表达期待对方反馈的意愿。
  • 语言风格：语气应亲切自然，体现朋友间的真诚交流，避免过于正式。

二、参考范文

Dear Tom,

I am writing, without hesitation, to share one of my favorite movies, Forrest Gump, with you, which is not only conducive to your study, but also beneficial to your life.

For one thing, the beautiful language in this original English movie may contribute to your study of English in listening, speaking, reading and writing. For another, the profound cultural elements implicit in the scenes will equip you with foreign cultural background and, above all, enrich your daily life.

Would you like to see this movie after my recommendation? Remember to tell me your opinion about it. I am looking forward to your early reply.

Yours,
Li Ming

三、写作技巧

  • 结构清晰：采用三段式，逻辑分明，内容完整。
  • 语言真诚：语气自然亲切，符合朋友间的交流习惯。
  • 实用句型：
    • 开头表明目的：
      “I am writing to you to recommend…”
      “I take great pleasure in recommending to you…”
    • 主体说明理由：
      “For one thing,… For another,…”
      “On the one hand,… On the other hand,…”
    • 结尾再次推荐并期待回复：
      “I am convinced that you will enjoy this movie after my enthusiastic recommendation.”
