一、审题谋篇

  • 写作类型：图画议论文

  • 话题类型：家庭教育 / 社会现象

  • 图画说明：

    • 图一：父亲坐在沙发上，一边看电视、玩手机，一边对儿子说“你给我好好学习”。
    • 图二：父亲与儿子一起在书桌前专注学习，桌上摆放书籍。
    • 图下文字：“与其只提要求，不如做个榜样”。

  • 写作要点：

    1. 简要描述图画内容；
    2. 阐释图画寓意；
    3. 发表个人评论。

二、参考范文

The two pictures above depict contrasting approaches to parenting. In the first illustration, a father idly watches television and plays on his phone while demanding that his son study hard. In the second, both father and son are absorbed in learning together at a desk filled with books. The caption below reads: “It is better to set an example than to make demands.”

Clearly, the cartoon emphasizes a common issue in family education: many parents neglect the influence of their own behavior on their children. While parents naturally wish for their children to succeed, they often rely solely on schools and society to provide a good education, overlooking their own role as primary role models. Failing to set a positive example can create an unhealthy family atmosphere, which may hinder a child’s growth and future development.

In my view, parental education plays a vital role in a child’s development. Therefore, parents should strive to create a supportive learning environment by actively demonstrating the values they wish to instill. Only through consistent effort and proper guidance can children be well-prepared for a promising future.

三、参考表达与框架

适用话题：家庭教育、榜样作用、亲子关系

参考句型与思路：

  • 描述对比：The two pictures present a sharp contrast between…
  • 点明主旨：The drawing highlights the importance of leading by example.
  • 分析影响：Parents serve as the first and most influential teachers in a child’s life.
  • 提出建议：It is essential for parents to participate actively in their children’s learning process and demonstrate the behaviors they encourage.

延伸思考：

  • 家长言行一致的重要性；
  • 家庭环境对孩子行为与态度的深远影响；
  • 如何在日常生活中实施“榜样教育”。
