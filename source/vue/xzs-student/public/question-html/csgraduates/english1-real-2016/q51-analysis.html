一、审题谋篇

  1. 写作类型：应用文——告示（Notice）
  2. 写作对象：新入学的国际学生
  3. 写作身份：大学图书馆管理员
  4. 核心任务：向国际新生介绍图书馆的相关信息与服务
  5. 内容要点：应涵盖图书馆的关键信息，如开放时间、借阅规则、资源设施、特色服务等，语言需简洁明了、正式友好。

二、参考范文

Notice

December 26, 2015

Welcome to our university! As the librarian, I am pleased to provide you with some key information about our library.

Our library offers a vast collection of books, journals, and online databases. You can access the digital resources and check your borrowing records through our library website. The opening hours are from 8:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. on weekdays, and from 9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. on weekends. To borrow books, please present your valid student ID card at the circulation desk. Additionally, we provide services like inter-library loans and research assistance.

We look forward to serving you and wish you a successful academic journey here.

Li Ming
