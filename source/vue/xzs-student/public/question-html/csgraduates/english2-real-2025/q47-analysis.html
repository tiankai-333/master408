【行文思路】

  1. 开头问候与目的：问候对方，并直接表明写信目的——介绍短剧并发出邀请。
  2. 主体内容介绍：清晰说明短剧的基本信息（如时间、地点）及其核心内容与特色。
  3. 结尾重申与期待：再次发出邀请，表达期待，并留下联系方式以便进一步沟通。

【参考范文】

Dear John,

I’m writing to tell you about a short play we’re planning and to invite you to join us. It’s based on the classic Chinese novel “Journey to the West”.

The play will be held at the Student Activity Center on June 20th at 7:00 PM. We think it’s a fantastic opportunity to share this story in a fun way. We would be delighted to have you as part of our team, perhaps in a role or helping backstage.

I really hope you can make it. Looking forward to your reply!

Best regards, Li Ming

【万能句型】

  • 开头表明目的：
    • I'm writing to tell you about... and to invite you to join us.
  • 介绍活动细节：
    • The play is based on... and will be held at (地点) on (日期).
    • It features...
  • 发出邀请：
    • We would be delighted to have you as part of our team.
    • Your participation would be a great addition.
  • 结尾表达期待：
    • I really hope you can make it.
    • Looking forward to your reply.
