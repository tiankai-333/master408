【行文思路】

  1. 描述图表：开门见山概括图表主题，并按照数据从高到低的顺序依次列出主要活动的占比。
  2. 分析原因：针对图中占比较高的主要活动（如看电视、散步）分析其流行的原因。
  3. 总结展望：总结当前现状，并对未来老年人休闲活动可能的发展趋势进行合理预测。

【参考范文】

The bar chart presents a survey on the main leisure activities of the elderly in a community. Astonishingly, 90.8% of the elderly choose to watch TV, followed by walking at 68.3%. Flower cultivation, reading, and chess playing occupy relatively smaller shares, accounting for 34.7%, 31.8%, and 18.4% respectively.

There are several reasons for this distribution. In the first place, watching TV is highly favored as it provides a great variety of entertainment and information, which can be easily accessed at home. In addition, walking is a simple and effective way to keep fit and is suitable for most elderly people. Last but not least, activities like chess playing, though less common, can stimulate their minds and offer opportunities for social interaction.

In conclusion, currently, watching TV dominates the leisure time of the elderly. However, with the promotion of healthy lifestyles and cultural activities, it is predicted that more diverse and active leisure pursuits will gradually gain popularity among the elderly in the future.
