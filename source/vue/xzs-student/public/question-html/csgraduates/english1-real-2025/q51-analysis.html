【参考范文】

Dear Paul,

I’m so delighted to hear that you are interested in our Handicraft Display Activity. I can’t wait to share the details with you.

The young craftsmen we’ve invited specialize in various fields and will showcase innovative techniques such as pottery, woodworking, and paper cutting. The event will take place in the Student Activity Center from 7:00 pm to 9:00 pm on December 28.

We would greatly appreciate your help in promoting the event. If you’re available, could you assist us by sharing information on social media, talking to classmates, and putting up posters around campus?

Thank you again for your enthusiasm. I look forward to your reply.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

【参考范文】

  1. 结构清晰：邮件依次回应了Paul的两个问题——活动内容与可提供的帮助，逻辑清晰。
  2. 信息具体：活动细节（时间、地点、内容）明确，请求的帮助（宣传、张贴海报）具体可行，让Paul能清楚了解并操作。
  3. 语言得体：用词礼貌、热情（如“delighted”, “greatly appreciate”），符合朋友兼同学间的沟通场景，也表达了感谢和期待。
  4. 字数合规：全文约100词，符合题目要求。

【写作要点提醒】

  • 直接回应：开头先感谢对方的兴趣，并直接回答问题。
  • 细节支撑：介绍活动时，用具体项目（如pottery, woodworking）增加吸引力。
  • 明确请求：寻求帮助时，提出清晰、具体的任务，便于对方响应。
  • 礼貌结尾：再次感谢并表达期待，保持邮件友好积极的基调。
