一、审题谋篇

本题为图画作文中的现象阐释题型。漫画通过具体场景和人物对话，生动揭示了一种普遍的生活态度差异。写作时应首先简要描述画面内容，进而阐释其深层寓意，并给出个人评论。

漫画主题分析： 画面描绘了一个打翻的水瓶，液体大部分洒出。面对同一情境，两个人反应截然不同：一人沮丧地感叹“全完了！”，另一人则微笑表示“幸好还剩点儿”。这鲜明对比折射出核心主题——心态的重要性，即我们应以积极乐观的态度面对生活中的得失。

文章结构建议：

  1. 第一段：客观描述漫画内容，聚焦两人面对困境的不同反应
  2. 第二段：阐释寓意，论证保持积极心态的价值和意义
  3. 第三段：提出个人观点，强调积极心态对解决问题的重要性

二、参考范文

As is vividly depicted in the cartoon, a knocked-over bottle lies on the ground with most of its contents spilled out. Confronted with this situation, two individuals react quite differently. One man, with a despairing expression, exclaims, “It’s all over!” while the other, wearing a gentle smile, remarks, “Fortunately, there’s still some left.”

Undoubtedly, the cartoon aims to convey a profound message about maintaining optimism in adversity. Firstly, life is inherently imperfect, and encountering setbacks is inevitable. To achieve our goals, we must confront challenges with positivity rather than succumb to negativity. Secondly, psychological research consistently demonstrates that an optimistic outlook enhances problem-solving abilities and overall well-being.

In conclusion, cultivating a positive mindset is essential for navigating life’s challenges. Only by embracing optimism can we effectively overcome obstacles and achieve personal growth.

  • 主题句：Undoubtedly, the cartoon aims to convey…
  • 论证方法：
