【审题谋篇】

本题要求撰写一封邀请邮件，对象为校内全体国际专家，目的是邀请其参加毕业典礼。写作时需包含时间、地点及其他相关典礼信息，字数控制在100词左右，语言应正式、礼貌。

写作内容可安排如下：

  1. 开头明确发出邀请，表明身份与目的；
  2. 主体部分清晰说明典礼的时间、地点及主要流程；
  3. 结尾表达诚意与期待，并请对方回复确认。

注意格式规范：称呼使用“Dear professors,”，结尾署名“Li Ming”，落款常用“Yours sincerely,”。

【参考范文】

Dear professors,

On behalf of the Students’ Union, I am writing to cordially invite you to attend our university’s graduation ceremony.

The ceremony will take place in the Sports Hall this Friday morning, from 9 a.m. to 11 a.m. The schedule includes an opening speech by our president and the presentation of diplomas by several honored guests.

Your presence would be a great honor for our graduates. We sincerely hope you can join us and would appreciate your confirmation at your earliest convenience.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming
