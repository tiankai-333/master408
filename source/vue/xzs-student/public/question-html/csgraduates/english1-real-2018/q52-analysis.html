【审题谋篇】

本题为提纲图画式作文。写作时应综合图画、文字和提纲进行审题。从文体上看，本题要求撰写一篇论说文。

图画描绘了一名大学生在选课系统前犹豫不决的场景。电脑一侧标注“知识新、重创新、有难度……”，人物一侧则标注“给分高、易通过、作业少……”。这反映出当前不少学生在选课时倾向于选择轻松易过的课程，而缺乏对长远发展的考量。然而，从个人成长与国家需求出发，大学生更应勇于选择具有挑战性、注重创新的课程。这类课程不仅能拓宽视野、增强综合能力，也有助于在高速发展的社会中提升竞争力，并为建设创新型国家贡献力量。

根据题目提纲，建议结构安排如下：

  • 第一段：简要描述图画内容，引出“学生在选课中面临两难选择”的主题；
  • 第二段：阐释选择具有创新性和挑战性课程的重要意义；
  • 第三段：提出个人观点，总结全文。

【参考范文】

As depicted in the cartoon, a college student is sitting in front of a computer, pondering over his course selection. On one side, the system suggests courses that are “knowledge-intensive, innovation-oriented, and challenging”; on the other, his own thoughts lean toward those that promise “high scores, easy passes, and minimal assignments.” Though simple, the image conveys a profound message about the choices students face.

Undoubtedly, the illustrator intends to emphasize the importance of embracing innovation and challenge. In other words, creativity is an indispensable quality for those who aspire to succeed. Throughout life, everyone encounters dilemmas, and the choices made during such critical moments can shape their future. Opting for a challenging path, despite its difficulties, often leads to growth and eventual achievement. In contrast, choosing the easier route may provide temporary comfort but can ultimately hinder personal development and fulfillment.

As college students, we will inevitably face difficult decisions. In these moments, we ought to remember that innovation is not just a skill, but a vital attitude toward life and a stepping stone to success. By fostering a spirit of creativity and courage, we can pave the way for a more promising future.
