【题目解析】

  1. 信件类型：这是一封回复朋友祝贺并给予建议的回信，属于半正式书信。
  2. 语气要求：语气应热情友好，体现感谢之意与分享精神。
  3. 内容要点：
    • 感谢对方的祝贺；
    • 针对翻译学习提出具体建议（2–3条）；
    • 表达进一步交流的意愿并期待回复。
  4. 格式规范：需包含称呼、正文、结尾敬语及署名，注意字数控制在100词左右。

【参考范文】

Dear Jack,

Thank you so much for your warm congratulations on my translation contest success.

As for advice, I believe the key lies in consistent practice and extensive reading. Try to engage with diverse texts—both Chinese and English—to enhance your sense of language. Also, learning about the cultures behind the languages can help you grasp nuanced meanings. Most importantly, keep practicing and stay curious.

I hope these suggestions help. Feel free to ask me more—I’m always happy to discuss!

Looking forward to your reply.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

  • It might be helpful to…
  • I strongly recommend that you…

▌结尾常用语句：

  • I hope you find my suggestions practical.
  • I’m more than willing to talk more if you need.
  • Feel free to reach out anytime for further discussion.
