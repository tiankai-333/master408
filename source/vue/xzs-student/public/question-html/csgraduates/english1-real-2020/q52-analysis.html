The illustration presents a thought-provoking contrast between two students. On the left, a girl remarks, “Only finishing early puts my mind at ease,” while diligently completing her assignments. In stark contrast, the boy on the right claims, “I won’t start until the last minute,” appearing relaxed and unconcerned. The caption below simply reads: “Habit.”

This picture conveys a profound message about the power of habits in shaping our lives. It highlights how the routines we cultivate—such as proactive planning versus chronic procrastination—can lead to vastly different outcomes. Good habits, like the girl’s early completion of tasks, not only reduce stress but also build self-discipline and efficiency. Conversely, the boy’s last-minute approach may lead to anxiety, rushed work, and underperformance.

In my view, developing positive habits is essential for personal and professional success. While it requires consistent effort and self-awareness, the long-term benefits—such as better time management, increased reliability, and inner calm—are invaluable. Schools and families should therefore emphasize habit formation from an early age, guiding young people to recognize that small, daily routines ultimately define the direction and quality of their lives.

【结构分析】

范文严格按照题目要求，采用了经典的三段式结构：

  • 第一段（描述图画 Brief Description）: 开篇直接点明图画的核心是对比（contrast）。然后用一句话分别描述了左右两幅漫画的人物、语言和状态，语言精炼，抓住了“尽早完成”和“拖延”的核心冲突。最后点出标题“习惯”，完成描述任务。
  • 第二段（阐释寓意 Interpret the Meaning）: 本段是论述的核心。第一句直接点明图画的深层寓意——习惯塑造人生的力量。第二句具体展开，指出“积极主动”和“慢性拖延”这两种习惯会导致截然不同的结果。第三、四句则分别论述两种习惯带来的具体影响（好处与坏处），使论证非常充实。
  • 第三段（给出评论 Give Your Comments）: 这一段是个人观点的延伸。首先直接表明立场（In my view），强调养成好习惯的重要性。然后客观地指出养成好习惯需要付出努力，并列举其长期价值。最后提出建议，将话题升华到教育和家庭层面，体现了思维的深度和广度。

【范文亮点详解】

  1. 逻辑清晰，结构严谨： 文章遵循“描述-阐释-评论”的流程，每段对应一个指令，无缝衔接，让阅卷老师一目了然。
  2. 语言准确且富有变化：
    • 开篇： “presents a thought-provoking contrast” （呈现了一个发人深省的对比），比直接说“There are two pictures”要高级得多。
    • 词汇： 使用了同义替换，如 “proactive planning” （主动规划）与 “chronic procrastination” （慢性拖延）形成对比；“self-discipline” （自律）、“efficiency” （效率）、“reliability” （可靠）等词汇准确且丰富。
    • 句式： 交替使用简单句和复合句（如由 not only... but also..., While... 引导的句子），体现了对英语句式的熟练驾驭能力。
  3. 论述深刻，立意升华： 文章没有停留在“不要拖延”的表面说教，而是上升到“习惯决定人生质量和方向”的高度。最后一句“small, daily routines ultimately define the direction and quality of their lives” （微小的日常习惯最终定义了他们人生的方向和质量）是全文的点睛之笔，极具哲理，容易获得高分。
