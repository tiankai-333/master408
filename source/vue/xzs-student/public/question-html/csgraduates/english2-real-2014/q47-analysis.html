【要点归纳】

  1. 题目要求写一封电子邮件，需包含书信基本格式（称呼、正文、结尾敬语、署名）。
  2. 收信人为未来室友，语气应亲切自然，体现友好与尊重。
  3. 内容需涵盖两方面：
    • 介绍个人生活习惯（可结合文化背景）；
    • 主动询问对方关于当地生活的建议，体现交流意愿。

【范文参考】

Dear John,

I’m Li Ming, your future roommate from China. I’m really excited to be sharing the apartment with you during my study abroad.

I’d like to share some of my living habits with you. I usually keep a regular schedule—going to bed and waking up early. I enjoy cooking Chinese meals at home, so I hope you don’t mind occasional cooking smells. Also, I like keeping shared spaces clean and tidy.

Since I’m new to your country, could you give me some advice on local customs or things I should know? Looking forward to your suggestions!

Best regards, Li Ming

【写作储备】

📌 生活习惯常用表达：

  • 良好习惯：keep a regular schedule（作息规律），maintain a balanced diet（饮食均衡），enjoy cooking at home（喜欢在家做饭），keep the room tidy（保持房间整洁）
  • 其他表达：be a morning person（习惯早起），be into fitness（喜欢健身），value quiet time（注重安静时间）

📌 询问建议常用句型：

  1. Could you share some advice on…?
  2. I’d appreciate it if you could tell me about…
  3. As I’m new here, any tips would be helpful.

📌 自我介绍常用句：

  1. I’m generally tidy and respectful of shared spaces.
  2. I’m easy-going and open to communicating about our living arrangements.
  3. Please feel free to let me know your preferences as well.
