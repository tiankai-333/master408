现象解读
从1990年至2010年，中国总人口呈温和增长，但人口分布发生了显著变化。城镇人口从3亿大幅上升至6.7亿，而乡村人口则从8.2亿下降至6.8亿。在此期间，城乡人口差距明显缩小。

范文参考
Between 1990 and 2010, China experienced a moderate increase in total population, yet a dramatic shift occurred in population distribution. Urban population rose significantly from 300 million to 670 million, while rural population declined from 820 million to 680 million.

This narrowing gap can be attributed to urbanization and unequal economic opportunities. Over these two decades, urban expansion led many rural residents to become city dwellers, either passively or by choice. At the same time, limited economic prospects in rural areas kept many peasants near the poverty line, motivating young and able-bodied individuals to migrate to cities for better livelihoods.

The rise in urban population reflects economic progress, yet challenges remain. Many newcomers struggle to integrate due to limited education and urban habits, leaving them marginalized despite urban prosperity. Therefore, beyond celebrating these figures, efforts must focus on fostering inclusive development for all urban residents.
