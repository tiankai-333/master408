【写作思路】

本题为图表描述类作文，建议采用经典的三段式结构：描述图表 → 分析原因 → 总结展望。

第一段：描述图表

  • 开篇点明图表内容：2012–2021年中国居民健康素养水平变化。
  • 概括整体趋势：持续稳步上升。
  • 选取关键数据说明变化，如起点（2012年，8.80%）、增速加快的转折年份（2016年，11.58%）和终点（2021年，25.40%）。

第二段：分析原因 从以下2–3个角度展开分析，每点简要说明：

  1. 国家政策推动：政府加大健康科普与公共卫生宣传。
  2. 媒体与技术发展：互联网、短视频等平台普及，健康知识获取更便捷。
  3. 公众健康意识增强：新冠疫情等公共卫生事件促使人们更加关注健康。
  4. 经济与教育水平提高：居民生活水平及受教育程度提升，更重视健康管理。

第三段：总结展望

  • 总结健康素养水平持续提高的趋势。
  • 展望未来，该趋势有望延续，对全民健康水平提升具有重要意义。

【参考范文】

The line chart clearly illustrates changes in the health literacy level of Chinese residents from 2012 to 2021. Overall, there was a consistent upward trend throughout this period. Starting at 8.80% in 2012, the rate grew gradually to 11.58% by 2016, after which it rose more rapidly, reaching 25.40% in 2021.

Several factors may explain this positive trend. Firstly, the government has implemented various health education campaigns to raise public awareness. Secondly, the widespread use of the internet and social media has made health information more accessible. Moreover, the COVID-19 pandemic heightened people’s attention to personal health and disease prevention.

In conclusion, the health literacy level of Chinese citizens has shown remarkable improvement over the past decade. This trend is likely to continue, contributing significantly to national public health.
