【参考范文】

Dear David,

I’m very glad to receive your letter asking for my advice on whether to go to the art exhibition or the robot show. In my opinion, compared with the robot show, the art exhibition would be a better choice. Here are the reasons.

Firstly, the art exhibition is an important tool for spreading new artistic ideas and promoting cultural development, and therefore can help you develop a deep understanding of culture and experience the emerging trend of art. Secondly, so far as I know, you have always been interested in art, and I’m sure the art exhibition will help you learn more about it.

I hope you find my proposal useful.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

【万能句】

一、开头段 – 表明来信并给予建议

  1. 提及收到咨询

    • I’m writing to share my thoughts on your dilemma about choosing between…
    • Thanks for reaching out to me for advice on whether to attend…
    • It’s great to hear from you! I understand it’s a tough choice between…

  2. 明确提出建议

    • If I were you, I would lean towards the [活动A] without hesitation.
    • After careful consideration, I believe the [活动A] is the more rewarding option for you.
    • While both events sound fascinating, my vote goes to the [活动A].

二、主体段 – 阐述理由

  1. 引出理由

    • The primary reason for my recommendation is that…
    • I have a couple of reasons to support my suggestion. First,…
    • This recommendation is mainly based on your personal interests and the event’s uniqueness.

  2. 阐述理由的万能角度

    • 从朋友兴趣出发：Given your passion for [艺术/科技], this exhibition/show is a perfect match for your interests.
    • 从活动独特性出发：This is a rare opportunity to experience [例如: cutting-edge AI technology / works by a renowned local artist], which might not come again soon.
    • 从个人收获出发：It will not only be entertaining but also greatly broaden your horizons in the field of [相关领域].
    • 从放松身心出发：I think a stroll through the art exhibition would be a more relaxing and inspiring way to spend your Sunday.

三、结尾段

  • I’m sure you’ll have a fantastic time there if you choose to go.
  • I hope this helps you make a decision. Let me know what you end up doing!
  • Whatever you choose, I’m looking forward to hearing about your experience!
