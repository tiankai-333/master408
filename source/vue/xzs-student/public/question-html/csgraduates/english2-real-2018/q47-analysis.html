【归纳要点】

  1. 格式要求：书信（道歉信），需包含称呼、正文、结尾敬语及署名。
  2. 语域把握：收信人为教授，语气应礼貌、尊敬；因属私人拜访，表达可适当自然，不必过于正式拘谨。
  3. 内容要点：
    • 表达歉意并简要说明取消原因；
    • 主动提出改期会面，表达期待。

【参考范文】

Dear Professor Smith,

I am writing to apologize for not being able to visit you as planned, since I have to cancel my travel plan for some reason. I am terribly sorry for any inconvenience this causes you and any changes that it necessitates in your current plans.

I have been looking forward to seeing you since we made the appointment. Unexpectedly, I was asked to substitute for a classmate who suddenly fell ill to attend an important meeting.

If it is possible, I would like to reschedule our appointment to a time convenient for you. Thank you for understanding in this matter.

Looking forward to your reply.

Sincerely yours,
Li Ming

【写作储备】

道歉信常用表达

  1. 开篇：说明写作目的

    • I want to write you a letter to apologize.
    • Please accept my sincere apology for…
    • Please accept this letter as my formal apology for what happened on…

  2. 中间段：说明理由 / 提出补救措施

    • It was not my intention to cause any damage or inconvenience.
    • I would try to offer an explanation for my behavior/what happened, although this is by no means an excuse.
    • Would it be convenient for you if…?
    • Would you prefer to…?

  3. 结尾：再次表示歉意，请求原谅

    • I sincerely thank you for hearing me out. Again, I am sorry for what happened.
    • Again, I deeply apologize for the problems my actions caused, and I hope I can work to make it right.
    • I am sorry to…, and hope that this has not caused you any great inconvenience.
