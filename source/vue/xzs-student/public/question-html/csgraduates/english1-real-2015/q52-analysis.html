一、审题谋篇

本题属于图画作文中的现象阐释题型。漫画展示了一种普遍的社会现象，并配有明确的文字说明“手机时代的聚会”。该图具有明显的讽刺意味，揭示了手机对人际交往的负面影响。

这一主题与2009年真题“网络的‘近’与‘远’”有相似之处，虽然当年考题提到网络利弊并存，但重点仍在其弊端上。因此，考生在理解与写作时并不困难。

建议采用三段式结构：

  • 第一段：简要描述图画内容。
  • 第二段：点明主题——手机对人际交往的不良影响，并进行阐述。
  • 第三段：针对图中现象提出建议，倡导合理使用手机，回归正常社交。

二、参考范文

Unfolded in the elaborately painted cartoon is a thought-provoking scene: four youngsters are sitting together for a gathering feast. However, much of their attention is absorbed by their mobile phones, completely forgetting the purpose of their meeting. As the caption states, this is a typical “Dinner party in the mobile era.”

This picture reflects a widespread phenomenon in contemporary society — excessive addiction to mobile phones. Indeed, we have entered an era where telecommunication dominates how we connect with others. While technology seems to bring people closer, we must also recognize its side effects: high-tech devices often hinder face-to-face communication. For instance, it is common to see people engrossed in their phones or tablets on buses, subways, or even while walking — reading novels, watching videos, or browsing the internet — even when they are accompanied by friends or family.

Clearly, a rational approach is needed to address this issue. The media should promote awareness of a healthy lifestyle, particularly among the young. Meanwhile, social activities — whether in communities or on campuses — ought to be encouraged to help people reengage with the real world.

三、写作技巧

第一段：简要描图

  • Unfolded in the elaborately painted cartoon is…
  • Just as is put in the caption of this cartoon,…

第二段：点明主题，举例论证

  • This picture is naturally associated with a widespread phenomenon in contemporary society —…
  • To be specific, in our daily life, it’s not uncommon that…

第三段：总结评论，提出建议

  • Evidently, we should take a rational attitude towards this issue, but how?
  • The media should promote the awareness of…
  • Meanwhile, social activities, either in communities or on campuses, should be launched so that people can…
