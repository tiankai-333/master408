【现象解读】

图表清晰展示了2013至2015年间我国博物馆数量与参观人次的变化趋势。两条折线均呈上升态势，反映出我国文化事业的蓬勃发展。

具体而言：

  • 参观人数（方形标记） 持续快速增长，从2013年的约6亿人次增至2015年的约8亿人次，表明公众的文化需求日益旺盛。
  • 博物馆数量（菱形标记） 虽整体增长，但增速在后期放缓，从快速增长过渡到相对稳定。这一变化与参观人数的持续高增长形成对比，提示了潜在的挑战：若参观热度不减，现有的博物馆资源未来可能面临“供不应求”的压力。

考生在论述时，可基于此数据变化阐发个人见解，或深入分析现象背后的成因。

【参考范文】

The given chart illustrates the consecutive growth in both the number of museums and museum visitors in China from 2013 to 2015. It is noteworthy that while the growth in museum numbers slowed down after 2014, the number of visitors maintained a strong upward trend.

This phenomenon can be attributed to several factors. Firstly, national efforts in promoting cultural undertakings have led to the construction of numerous museums, many of which offer free admission to the public. Secondly, China’s rapid economic development has underpinned this cultural progress. As living standards improve, public demand for diverse spiritual enrichment grows, making museum visits an increasingly popular choice.

However, this encouraging trend also presents challenges. The slowing growth in museum supply may struggle to meet the rapidly increasing and diversifying public demand for exhibitions. To address this, museums can enhance their efficiency and reach by embracing digital technologies, such as developing online “e-museums.” Through digital images and interactive videos, visitors can access countless exhibits globally and enjoy fresh experiences. It is believed that through such joint efforts, China’s museums will become a stronger driving force for cultural development and an important means of enhancing public well-being, thereby further boosting socio-economic progress.
