【范文】

In the picture, two teams of dragon boat racers are paddling vigorously through the water, with crowds both on the bridge and along the banks cheering them on. Delighted by this scene, an elderly lady says to her spouse, “It is really wonderful that the dragon boat racing in our village is becoming increasingly popular and exciting!”

The picture demonstrates the public’s growing interest in traditional Chinese culture and customs. Nowadays, many Chinese people participate in various cultural activities during festivals or in daily life. Through these events, they not only gain enjoyment but also deepen their understanding of history and culture. Moreover, such activities contribute to rural revitalization. For instance, villagers promote intangible cultural heritage through sports events, art performances, or craft fairs, attracting visitors from near and far.

In summary, holding cultural events in rural areas is a commendable and sustainable way to preserve time-honored traditions and promote local development. As the younger generation, we also bear the responsibility to protect Chinese culture and contribute to the prosperity of our hometowns.
