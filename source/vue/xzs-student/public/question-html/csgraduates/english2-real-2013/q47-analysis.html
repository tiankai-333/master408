【要点归纳】

  1. 文体类型：电子邮件（书信类），需注意格式完整，包括称呼、正文、结尾敬语及署名。
  2. 语言风格：收信人为同班同学，语言应亲切、自然，带有鼓励和号召性，避免过于正式或生硬。
  3. 内容要点：
    • 明确告知慈善义卖的具体信息（如时间、地点）。
    • 清晰说明活动的目的和意义（帮助对象、善款用途）。
    • 真诚鼓励并邀请同学们积极参与。

【参考范文】

Dear classmates,

I’m writing to let you know that our class will hold a charity sale on September 23, from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m., in the school auditorium. The event aims to raise funds for children in need, and all proceeds will be donated to the Hope Project to support 15 kids from financially struggling families.

Your participation is crucial to making this a success! We’ll have a variety of items on sale, including secondhand books and stationery at great prices. Come along, find something you like, and contribute to a meaningful cause.

Looking forward to seeing you there!

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

【写作储备】

1. 相关词汇

  • charity sale / fundraising sale：慈善义卖
  • secondhand / pre-owned items：二手物品
  • donated goods：捐赠物品
  • raise funds / collect donations：筹集资金
  • proceeds：收入，收益
  • meaningful cause：有意义的事业

2. 常用表达

  • 开头信息告知：

    • I’m writing to inform you that… / to let you know that…
    • Our class is organizing a charity sale on [date] at [venue].

  • 说明活动目的：

    • The event aims to raise money for…
    • All proceeds will go to [charity/ cause] to support…

  • 鼓励参与：

    • Your support/participation would mean a lot.
    • We would be delighted if you could join us.
    • Come along and show your support!

  • 结尾呼吁：

    • Looking forward to your participation!
    • Hope to see you there!
    • Let’s make a difference together!
