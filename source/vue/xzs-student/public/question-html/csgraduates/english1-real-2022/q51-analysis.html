行文思路解析

  1. 第一段：告知事项，发出邀请

    • 明确告知教授关于国际创新竞赛的信息
    • 直接发出参赛邀请

  2. 第二段：介绍比赛信息，说明邀请理由

    • 阐述比赛目的和主题
    • 说明参赛形式和评选标准
    • 强调教授团队的专业优势
    • 提及已确认参赛的团队情况

  3. 第三段：告知回复方式和截止日期

    • 明确回复截止时间
    • 提供进一步咨询的渠道

参考范文

Dear Professor Smith,

The annual International Innovation Contest organized by my university is scheduled for late next month (24th-30th). We cordially invite you and your students to participate as a team.

This contest aims to inspire innovative solutions to real-world problems. This year’s theme focuses on urban waste disposal and recycling. Each team of up to four members will develop a detailed proposal, which will be evaluated by an independent jury based on originality and practicality. Given your expertise in urban planning, your team could offer valuable insights into sustainable waste management. Over 15 teams from prestigious global universities have already confirmed participation, making this a rewarding opportunity for academic exchange.

Please inform us of your decision by replying to this email before Friday, September 25th. Should you require any additional information, do not hesitate to contact me.

Yours sincerely, Li Ming
