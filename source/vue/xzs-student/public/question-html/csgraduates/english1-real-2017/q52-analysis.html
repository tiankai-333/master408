一、审题谋篇

  • 写作类型： 议论文
  • 话题类型： 人生哲理类
  • 图画说明：
    • 图一：一个人拥有很多书却不去读。
    • 图二：一个人设定目标，争取今年读完20本书。
  • 核心要点：
    1. 简要描述图画内容。
    2. 阐释图画的深层寓意。
    3. 发表个人评论。

二、范文参考

Portrayed in the two cartoons is a thought-provoking scenario. In the left one, a young man, comfortably lounging on a chair, looks contentedly at his huge bookshelf filled with books, seemingly satisfied with mere ownership. Conversely, the individual in the right drawing sits at a desk, determined to achieve a specific goal: finishing 20 books within the year.

Through this contrast, the cartoonist highlights the disparity between possessing resources (“having books”) and taking concrete action (“reading books”). It reminds us that in our fast-paced society, continuous learning and knowledge updating are crucial for success, which can only be attained through dedicated reading. However, objectives remain mere fantasies without determined action. Often, people fail not because challenges are insurmountable, but because they cease to persevere.

In conclusion, meaningful achievement stems from consistent effort and a persistent spirit, not just from potential or possessions. Action invariably speaks louder than words. Understanding and applying this principle in one’s studies and work will undoubtedly yield significant benefits.
