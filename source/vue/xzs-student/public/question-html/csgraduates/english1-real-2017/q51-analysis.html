一、审题谋篇

  • 写作类型：电子邮件
  • 写作对象：新来的澳大利亚教授 James Cook
  • 写作要点：
    1. 推荐所在城市的旅游景点
    2. 给出推荐理由
    3. 语气礼貌、表达自然
    4. 注意格式规范，落款为“Li Ming”

二、范文参考

Dear James Cook,

Welcome to China! I’m writing to recommend some scenic spots in Beijing so that you can have a wonderful time here.

First of all, you could visit the Summer Palace and the Forbidden City, which are renowned for their historic architecture and rich cultural heritage. After that, it is advisable to go to Wangfujing Street, where you can taste delicious local snacks while experiencing traditional culture. Finally, you may enjoy the National Museum, where a variety of traditional art exhibitions are being held.

What do you think of my suggestions?

I sincerely hope that you will enjoy your stay in Beijing, and it would be my pleasure to be your guide.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming
