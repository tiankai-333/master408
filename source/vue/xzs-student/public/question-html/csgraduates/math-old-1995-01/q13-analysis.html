∬Σ​zdS=∬D​x2+y2​⋅2​dxdy=2​∬D​x2+y2​dxdy.

∬D​x2+y2​dxdy=∫−π/2π/2​∫02cosθ​r⋅rdrdθ=∫−π/2π/2​∫02cosθ​r2drdθ.

∫−π/2π/2​38cos3θ​dθ=38​∫−π/2π/2​cos3θdθ.

∫−π/2π/2​cos3θdθ=2∫0π/2​cos3θdθ=2⋅32​=34​.

∬D​x2+y2​dxdy=38​⋅34​=932​.
