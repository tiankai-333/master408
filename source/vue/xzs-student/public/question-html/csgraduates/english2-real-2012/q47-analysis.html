【要点归纳】

  1. 文体与格式：本题要求写一封正式投诉邮件，需包含完整的书信格式（称呼、正文、结尾敬语、署名）。
  2. 语气与态度：收信对象为客服人员，语气应礼貌得体，既要清晰表达不满，也要尊重对方工作，避免过于生硬或情绪化。
  3. 内容要点：
    • 简要说明购买情况（产品名称、购买时间与渠道）；
    • 具体描述产品存在的问题；
    • 明确提出解决方案（如换货、维修或退款）；
    • 结尾表达感谢或期望，并留下联系方式。

【范文参考】

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am writing to express my dissatisfaction with an electronic dictionary (Model P90o) that I purchased from your online store the day before yesterday. Unfortunately, it has not been functioning properly. The device frequently shuts down on its own, which severely affects its usability.

I would appreciate it if you could arrange for a replacement or repair as per the warranty policy. If neither option is feasible, I would request a full refund.

Thank you for your attention to this matter. Please feel free to contact me at 12345678 if further details are needed.

Yours faithfully,
Zhang Wei

常见电子词典问题表达：

  • short battery life 电池续航短
  • unable to update 无法更新系统
  • stuck key / keys difficult to press 按键卡顿/难以按下
  • broken screen 屏幕破损
  • frequent application crash 程序频繁闪退

投诉信常用句型：

  1. 开头表达不满：

    • I wish to complain about…
    • I am writing to express my dissatisfaction with…
    • I must bring to your attention a problem with…

  2. 描述问题：

    • It is frustrating to find that…
    • I am disappointed because the product does not work as expected.
    • The device malfunctions frequently, which is highly inconvenient.

  3. 提出诉求：

    • I would appreciate it if you could replace/repair/refund…
    • I request that you resolve this issue at your earliest convenience.

  4. 结尾礼貌提醒：

    • I trust you will give this matter your prompt attention.
    • I look forward to your reply and a satisfactory solution.

改写说明：

  • 结构和格式标准化：将原文要点、范文、写作储备等内容按标准书信解析结构分段整理，条理更清晰，便于阅读和查找。
  • 表达和逻辑优化：对原文解析和例句进行语言润色，调整部分表述，使逻辑更顺畅、语气更正式。
  • 内容补充与细化：在常用表达和问题类型部分适当补充和细化内容，增强实用性和指导性。
