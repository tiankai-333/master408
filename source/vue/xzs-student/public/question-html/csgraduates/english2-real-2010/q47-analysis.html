解析

一、要点归纳

  1. 应用文类型：书信。需注意格式完整，包括称呼、正文、结尾敬语及署名。
  2. 语言风格：收信人为美国同事，用语不必过于正式，保持礼貌、亲切即可。
  3. 内容要点：
    • 表达对美国同事热情接待的感谢；
    • 为使感谢更自然，可适当回顾对方的款待细节，提及在对方帮助下的收获；
    • 最后诚挚邀请对方在合适的时候访问中国。

二、范文参考

Dear [Friend’s Name],

Thank you very much for your warm reception during my visit to the U.S. as a delegate of the Sino-American cultural exchange program. Your thoughtful arrangements made me feel completely at home. With your detailed explanations of American customs, I was able to quickly overcome culture shock and gain a deeper understanding of your culture. I truly appreciate everything you did for me.

I sincerely welcome you to visit China in due course. It would be my great pleasure to reciprocate your kindness and show you around. I am sure you will have a wonderful experience and gain rich insights into Chinese culture.

Looking forward to your visit!

Yours sincerely,
Zhang Wei

三、写作储备

1. 传达谢意

  • express gratitude 表达感恩
  • Gratitude is the sign of noble souls. 感恩是高尚灵魂的象征。
  • With sincere appreciation, I would like to thank you. 我要带着真诚的谢意感谢你。
  • Life is so much easier with great friends like you. 有你这么好的朋友，生活变得更加轻松。
  • Your support has made me a strong and driven individual, and I will forever be grateful to you. 你的支持让我成为一个坚强而奋发向上的人，我将永远对你心存感激。

2. 感谢信常用表达

  • 开头语：
    • I really appreciate your hospitality during my visit to…
    • Thank you for all the help you have given me with my…
    • I wish to express my heartfelt thanks to you for…
    • I want you to know how much I appreciate your…
  • 发展部分：
    • You helped me to…
    • You taught me to…
  • 结束语：
    • I truly appreciate your help.
    • Thanks again for your hospitality.
    • Thank you for your consideration.
