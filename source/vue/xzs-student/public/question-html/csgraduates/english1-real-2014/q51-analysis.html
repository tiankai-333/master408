一、审题谋篇

本题要求写一封建议信，收信人为大学校长，属于正式书信文体。写作时需注意语言规范，避免使用缩写、省略或口语化表达。

书信结构建议：

  • 称呼： 使用正式尊称，如 “Dear Mr. President,”。
  • 正文： 建议采用三段式结构：
    1. 开头段： 简要自我介绍，并说明写信目的。
    2. 主体段： 针对如何改善学生体质，提出2–3条具体可行的建议。
    3. 结尾段： 表达期望，如希望建议被考虑，并期待回复。
  • 落款： 使用正式结束语，如 “Yours sincerely,” “Sincerely yours,” 等，署名应为 “Li Ming”。

二、参考范文

Dear Mr. President,

I am a student of our university. I am writing to propose some practical suggestions for improving students’ physical health.

Firstly, the university could encourage regular exercise by providing more sports facilities and organizing fitness activities. Secondly, promoting balanced diets and offering healthier food options on campus would also help enhance students’ well-being.

I hope you will consider these suggestions. Thank you for your time and attention.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

三、常用句型

  1. I am writing to propose some practical suggestions for…
  2. It would be beneficial if…
  3. I hope you will consider these suggestions.
  4. Thank you for your time and attention.
