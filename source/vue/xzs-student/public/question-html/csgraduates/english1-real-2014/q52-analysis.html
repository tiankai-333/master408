Directions

Write an essay of 160–200 words based on the following drawing. In your essay, you should:

  1. Describe the drawing briefly,
  2. Interpret its intended meaning, and
  3. Give your comments.

You should write neatly on the ANSWER SHEET. (20 points)

一、审题谋篇

2014年考研英语大作文难度适中，紧扣社会热点——亲情与感恩。与2005年“赡养老人”主题的漫画相比，本图描述更为直观，写作难度有所降低。建议采用三段式结构展开：

  • 第一段：图画描述
    简要对比左右两图：三十年前，年轻的母亲牵着年幼的女儿；如今，女儿已长大，搀扶着年迈的母亲。重点突出右侧画面，强调子女对父母的感恩与陪伴。

  • 第二段：阐释内涵
    点明图画寓意——子女应关爱父母，并可从正反两方面展开论证：既可正面倡导孝道风尚，也可反面批评忽视父母关爱的现象。

  • 第三段：个人评论
    强调年轻人应把孝敬父母视为道德责任，并提出具体建议。唯有如此，家庭才能和睦，社会才能和谐。

二、参考范文

As can be seen, the drawing vividly reflects the enduring love between a mother and her daughter. In the left panel, a young and amiable mother is holding her little daughter’s hand. In the right one, the daughter, now grown up, supports her aged mother, just as her mother did thirty years ago.

As the caption “相携” suggests, the message conveyed here is self-evident: we ought to show continuous care and love for our parents. However, many people fail to fulfill this duty. With the accelerating pace of life, traditional moral values are fading. For instance, many young people prioritize careers over family. They often move away for work and become too busy to accompany their parents, leaving them in empty nests. Moreover, some attempt to replace genuine care with money or gifts. Although material support can improve parents’ living conditions, what the elderly need most is companionship—a simple phone call or a warm meal together.

In conclusion, nothing brings parents greater happiness than their children’s filial love. We should make time for family reunions whenever possible, and never forget to call our parents regularly. Only by being truly grateful and attentive can we ensure our parents lead a happy and fulfilling life.
