【行文思路】

  1. 第一段：描述图表

    • 开篇点明图表主题：某高校劳动实践课学生主要收获调查。
    • 有序呈现数据：按照收获比例从高到低描述，突出“学习知识”和“提升动手能力”这两项主要收获，并提及“心情舒畅”和“合作能力”等其他发现。

  2. 第二段：评论与阐释

    • 过渡引出原因分析。
    • 从两个层面展开分析：
      • 学生自身需求：面对激烈的就业竞争，学生认识到需要具备课本外的综合知识与实践技能以增强竞争力。
      • 外部环境推动：学校与社会媒体的宣传倡导，鼓励学生全面发展，走出“象牙塔”。

  3. 第三段：总结与建议

    • 总结劳动实践课的多元价值。
    • 向高校提出建议：应提供更多此类实践机会，并可与单纯课堂学习的局限性进行对比，使建议更具说服力。

【参考范文】

The bar chart clearly illustrates the primary benefits students gained from labor practice courses in a university. According to the survey, an overwhelming majority of students (91.3%) reported acquiring relevant knowledge, followed closely by 84.8% who felt their practical skills were enhanced. Meanwhile, over half of the respondents (54.4%) experienced improved mood, while a smaller yet significant proportion (32.6%) noted enhanced cooperation abilities.

Several factors contribute to this phenomenon. Firstly, facing an increasingly competitive job market, students are more aware of the necessity to equip themselves with comprehensive knowledge and hands-on skills beyond textbooks, which largely explains the high percentages associated with knowledge acquisition and practical ability improvement. Secondly, universities and media have been actively promoting holistic education, encouraging students to step out of their “ivory towers,” develop their potential, and enhance mutual cooperation.

In conclusion, active participation in practical courses benefits students in multiple ways. Therefore, it is suggested that universities should provide more such opportunities to foster students’ overall development, rather than confining learning solely to classroom-based theoretical instruction.
