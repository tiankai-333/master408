【行文思路】

此类邮件旨在提出计划并征求对方意见，结构清晰，建议采用以下三步法：

1. 开头

  • 礼貌问候，建立友好基调。
  • 直接说明邮件目的——为即将开展的调查提出初步计划。

2. 正文

  • 清晰、有条理地阐述具体计划步骤，可使用“首先…其次…最后…”等逻辑连接词。
  • 计划内容应具体可行，例如：实地考察、问卷调查、专家访谈等。

3. 结尾

  • 明确征求对方意见，体现合作精神。
  • 使用标准邮件结束语和签名。

【参考范文】

Dear Jack,

I am very glad to have the chance to work with you on the survey of protection of old houses in Anren Town. And I am writing to propose a plan.

My plan includes the following two steps. The first one, I suppose, is that we should get into contact with the local government to inquire about how many old houses have been preserved. With that figure in mind, then, we can set up a specific period of time to visit those houses and learn about their conditions. When we get done with this, we can move on to the next stage.

What do you think of my plan? Please feel free to contact me if you have any other ideas. Looking forward to your reply.

Yours,
Li Ming

【万能句型】

以下句型可根据需要灵活运用于同类合作计划邮件中：

1. 开头句

  • I hope this email finds you well.
    （希望您一切安好。）——通用礼貌的开场白。
  • I’m writing to you regarding our upcoming survey on…
    （我写这封邮件是关于我们即将进行的关于…的调查。）
  • As we are going to collaborate on the survey about…, I’d like to propose a preliminary plan.
    （鉴于我们将合作进行关于…的调查，我想提出一个初步计划。）

2. 提出计划

  • Here is a draft plan for your consideration.
    （这是一个计划草案，供您参考。）
  • My preliminary suggestions are as follows.
    （我的初步建议如下。）
  • To ensure the success of our survey, I suggest we take the following steps:
    （为保障调查顺利，我建议采取以下步骤：）
  • 列举步骤常用词：
    • First and foremost,… / Firstly,…
      （首先…）
    • Additionally,… / Secondly,…
      （此外…/ 其次…）
    • Finally,… / Lastly,…
      （最后…）

3. 征求对方意见

  • I would appreciate it if you could let me know your thoughts.
    （如您能分享想法，我将不胜感激。）
  • What do you think about this plan?
    （您认为这个计划如何？）
  • Please feel free to share your suggestions or make any modifications.
    （请随时提出建议或进行调整。）
  • Your feedback is highly valued.
    （非常重视您的反馈。）

4. 结尾句

  • Looking forward to your reply.
    （期待您的回复。）
  • I’m awaiting your feedback.
    （等待您的反馈。）
  • Best regards, / Sincerely, / Yours,
    （此致敬礼）——标准结束语。
